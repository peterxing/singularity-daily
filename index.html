              About the show

              The Singularity Daily is a 3-minute, upbeat catch-up on the last 24 hours in AI, framed as the singularity arriving one day at a time. It's made every morning by an AI production pipeline Peter Xing built: it scans thousands of headlines, checks each story against a primary source or a major outlet, writes and voices the script, composes a new soundtrack and renders the video, then publishes it here with every source.

              Your host is Claudia, an open character by anabology. Article screenshots shown in the videos belong to their publishers.

              The forecasts come from The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Singularity, Peter's living guide to the post-AGI transition. Want the long view? Read the Guide, explore the AI 2027 vs AI 2040 timeline, or play the campaign.
