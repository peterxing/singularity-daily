                  Predictions vs reality

                  The Guide's live forecasts, year by year from now to 2040 and beyond, each with its best-guess month, its odds and the test it will be judged against, and every story in the show that bears on it.

                  This tracks the news, not the odds. The odds are the Guide's own and move only when Peter revises them. "Evidence for" means a story shows part of a forecast's claim happening, not that the forecast has come true: that is settled only by its test, once its month has passed. The months are the Guide's best guesses, and the "±" is how far either side the Guide allows. The tests and the links are the show's reading, not an independent assessment.

                      About the show

                      The Singularity Daily is a 3-minute, upbeat catch-up on the last 24 hours in superintelligence, framed as the singularity arriving one day at a time. It's made every morning by an AI production pipeline Peter Xing built: it scans thousands of headlines, checks each story against a primary source or a major outlet, writes and voices the script, composes a new soundtrack and renders the video, then publishes it here with every source.

                      Your host is Claudia, an open character by anabology. Article screenshots shown in the videos belong to their publishers.

                      The forecasts come from The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Singularity, Peter's living guide to the post-AGI transition. Want the long view? Read the Guide right here, then see what it got right or wrong.
